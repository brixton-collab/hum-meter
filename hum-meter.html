The Hum Meter

Hum one note through your swing. Where it breaks is where your tension is.

  1. Put your phone on the ground next to your ball, screen up.
  2. Tap start, then hum one note — and keep it going through the ball. Don’t stop when you hit it.
  3. Pick it up and look. It stops on its own when you go quiet.
