The Hum Meter

How steady is your hum through a golf swing? Hum one note and find out.

  1. Put your phone on the ground next to your ball, screen up.
  2. Tap start, then hum one note — all the way through your backswing, the ball, and your finish. Don’t stop when you hit it.
  3. Pick it up and look. It stops on its own when you go quiet.

No club handy? Hum one steady note for a few seconds. It still scores it.
